Collection

SATU

Our first collection. Six pieces in one material, one finish and one way of building.

Satu means one in Indonesian. One material, one system, one idea carried through every piece. The collection is made from repurposed teak, timber that has already lived a life, often in old joglo houses, and given another through Danish design and Indonesian hands.

Every piece is modular and knock-down. It travels flat, comes apart without damage, and goes back together as it was. If one part reaches its end, you replace the part, not the piece.

The surface is finished with Shou Sugi Ban: the teak is darkened with flame and finished with natural oil. What is left is the material itself: honest, tactile, and made to age well.

Materials from another life. Designed for another life.
