Why we exist

Hotels want Danish design. Then they fill the room with plastic.

Most hospitality furniture is bought, used for a few years and thrown away. We think the places that care most about how they look should also care how long their furniture lives.

So we start with teak that has already lived once, often in old joglo houses. We design every piece to ship flat, bolt together on site and be repaired part by part.

Our mission

Reuse beats recycling.

A bottle that gets refilled does more good than one that gets melted down. Furniture works the same way: the longer a piece stays in use, the less needs to be made again.

That idea shapes everything we design, and where we want to go next.
